# Code of Conduct

## The standard

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Read it there; it is the authoritative text and is reproduced in full on that page.

In short: everyone taking part — in issues, pull requests, reviews and any other project
space — is expected to behave respectfully. Harassment, personal attacks, and demeaning
or discriminatory conduct are not acceptable, and neither is publishing someone's private
information without their permission. Disagreement about technical questions is expected
and welcome; hostility toward people is not.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, and whenever someone is representing the project in
public.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer,
[@vschernoff](https://github.com/vschernoff), by GitHub direct message.

Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer will acknowledge receipt, look into
what happened, and respond with whatever action is warranted — which may be a private
request to change behaviour, removal of a comment, or a block from the repository.
Someone who is the subject of a report is told what was raised and given a chance to
respond, except where doing so would put the reporter at risk.

## A note on scale

This is a small project with a single maintainer, so enforcement is one person's
judgement rather than a committee's. That is stated plainly rather than dressed up: if
you are uncomfortable raising something with the maintainer directly, say so in your
report and it will be taken into account.
